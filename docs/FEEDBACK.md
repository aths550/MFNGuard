## What we heard

- **UI polish and visuals** (F2, F14, F43): testers asked for more work on the interface and workflow, and one asked for graphics.
- **Mobile layout** (F40): the one concrete problem reported. Effects and cards overshadowed the content on mobile, so mobile optimization is needed.
- **Errors:** no other errors or broken flows were reported; the remaining responses said none or left it blank.
- **Lower clarity scores** (F4, F5, F7): rated 3/5 with no explanation given, so onboarding wording may be worth tightening.
- **Unexplained "No" on result clarity** (F21, F41): neither gave a reason, and their other answers were positive, so this may be a misread of the question. Consider rewording it in the next form.
- **Positive comments:** one tester said the problem the product solves is real; others left short approvals.
